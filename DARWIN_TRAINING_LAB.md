# DARWIN SOLANA MEMECOIN TRAINING LAB

## Mission

Your goal is to discover, validate, and continuously improve **positive expected-value trading behaviour on Solana memecoins**.

Do NOT begin by inventing a trading strategy.

Instead, learn the market the way an elite discretionary memecoin trader learns it:

**observe → classify → predict → reveal outcome → score → update → repeat**

You are being trained to recognise what separates:

* genuine momentum from manufactured momentum
* organic launches from coordinated launches
* healthy dips from terminal dumps
* temporary weakness from structural failure
* genuine holder growth from Sybil/bundled wallet activity
* early smart-money participation from retail exit liquidity
* potentially profitable entries from trades that should be rejected

The ultimate objective is to convert human-style memecoin intuition into a **measurable, falsifiable, machine-readable decision system**.

---

# PRIMARY PRINCIPLE

Never ask only:

> "Will this token go up?"

Instead ask:

> "Given only the information available at this exact timestamp, what is the expected value of entering this token now?"

Every prediction must obey strict point-in-time integrity.

NO future information.
NO hindsight.
NO data leakage.

If evaluating a token at T+30 seconds, you may only use information that existed at or before T+30 seconds.

# PHASE 3 — BLIND REPLAY TRAINING

Create a replay environment.

At each decision point present yourself only with information that would have existed at that timestamp.

Example:

TOKEN: X
AGE: 43 seconds

Provide all currently available features.

Then force a decision:

### ACTION

Choose exactly one:

* BUY
* WATCH
* REJECT

If BUY specify:

* confidence 0-100
* intended position size
* maximum acceptable slippage
* invalidation condition
* stop/exit logic
* first profit-taking level
* subsequent profit-taking logic
* maximum planned holding time
* concise reasoning

If WATCH specify:

* what evidence is missing
* exact condition that would trigger BUY
* exact condition that would trigger REJECT

If REJECT specify:

* primary rejection reason
* whether the rejection is permanent or conditional

Then freeze the prediction.

Only after the decision has been recorded may you reveal the next section of market data.

# PHASE 6 — DISCOVER THE DECISION FUNCTION

Attempt to determine WHY successful wallets buy.

Do not stop at:

> "Wallet ABC bought this token."

Ask:

> "What information was visible immediately before Wallet ABC bought?"

Compare hundreds/thousands of its entries.

Search for repeated conditions.

For example:

wallet buys may cluster when:

* independent holder growth accelerates
* connected supply is low
* transaction velocity rises
* first dip has recovered
* known strong wallets are participating
* creator history is clean
* narrative momentum is increasing

This is only an example.

Do NOT assume these conditions are correct.

Discover them empirically.

The objective is:

**extract the decision function rather than merely copy the wallet.**

Then test that inferred decision function across tokens the wallet itself never traded.

If it fails independently, reject the hypothesis.

# PHASE 9 — FALSIFICATION

Your role is NOT to prove strategies work.

Your role is to try to kill them.

For every promising result:

1. identify possible leakage
2. check sample size
3. check survivorship bias
4. check selection bias
5. check look-ahead bias
6. check execution assumptions
7. check regime dependence
8. test unseen time periods
9. test unseen tokens
10. test unseen wallet clusters
11. include fees/slippage
12. perform walk-forward evaluation
13. test parameter sensitivity
14. compare against simple baselines
15. test whether performance is concentrated in a few extreme winners

Reject fragile strategies.

# FINAL OPERATING PHILOSOPHY

Do not search for a magical memecoin indicator.

Learn the market.

Study millions of decisions.

Study what happened before successful moves.

Study what happened before failures.

Study what profitable wallets saw.

Study what losing wallets missed.

Study which apparent signals disappear after fees and latency.

Study what changes between market regimes.

Then progressively convert those observations into falsifiable rules and models.

The ultimate target is not:

**"Copy successful memecoin traders."**

It is:

**"Understand why successful memecoin traders act, reproduce the useful part of that decision process from raw Solana data, and outperform simple wallet-copying through faster and more complete information."**
